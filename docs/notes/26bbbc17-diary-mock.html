Diary tab: design mock

A new Diary tab listing Nick's sessions with Michael Taft, Vince Horn and Emily Horn, and the Friday Pragmatic Dharma teacher training. Session times are real ones from his calendar. The counts are made up for the mock. Balla Bot supplies the session list. The app shows the list, counts sessions, and lets him write up or log a sit after each one. Reminders still come from Balla Bot on Telegram, so the app sends no alerts of its own.

"Today" in this mock is Wed 18 Nov 2026. Uses the app's own colours, card shapes and navigation. Use the button at the top right to switch to the light theme.
